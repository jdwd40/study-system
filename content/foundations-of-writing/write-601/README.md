# Revision, Feedback and a Finished Piece

Finish a small piece through deliberate revision rather than endless polishing. Planned lessons: Structural Revision before Line Editing (30 min); Using Feedback without Losing Your Voice (30 min); Final Edit and Before-and-After Review (45 min).

## Learning Objectives

- Prioritise meaning and structure before sentence edits and proofreading.
- Translate specific reader feedback into justified revisions while retaining your intent.
- Finish a 400–700 word piece and explain three revisions using before-and-after evidence.
