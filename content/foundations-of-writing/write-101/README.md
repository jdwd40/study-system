# Purpose, Reader and the Writing Process

Start with the reader and the intended effect, then separate planning, drafting and revising. Planned lessons: Purpose and Reader (25 min); Generating and Selecting Ideas (25 min); Drafting without Premature Editing (30 min).

## Learning Objectives

- Identify a specific reader, purpose and desired response.
- Write a one-sentence brief and select relevant ideas.
- Produce a short baseline draft and distinguish planning, drafting and revision.
