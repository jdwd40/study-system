# Clear Sentences and Precise Language

Make meaning easy to follow without flattening voice. Planned lessons: Sentence Boundaries and Punctuation (30 min); Strong Verbs and Precise Words (25 min); Rhythm, Emphasis and Concision (30 min).

## Learning Objectives

- Repair fragments, run-ons and ambiguous references in context.
- Use punctuation, verb tense and agreement to support intended meaning.
- Revise vague or overloaded sentences using precise nouns, verbs and varied rhythm.
