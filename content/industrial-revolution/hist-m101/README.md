# Britain Before Industrialisation

The conditions of Britain before industrialisation: agriculture, population, trade, empire, finance, institutions, scientific culture, energy, transport and proto-industrialisation — and the competing explanations for why Britain was unusually well placed.

## Learning Objectives

- Build a working understanding of agriculture, population, trade, empire, finance, institutions, scientific culture, energy, transport and proto-industrialisation in pre-industrial Britain
- Record competing explanations and historiographical disagreements about why Britain industrialised first
- Distinguish established evidence from likely interpretation, contested interpretation and speculation
