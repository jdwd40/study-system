# Scope portfolio project 1

## Objective

Understand and apply scope portfolio project 1 in a reliable AI automation and systems-integration workflow.

## Content

**Understand**

The Lead Management Assistant is a demonstrator, not a claim of autonomous sales. Choose one narrow customer journey: receive an enquiry, extract key details, look up an existing contact, suggest a category and next action, draft a response, and wait for human approval. Write acceptance criteria before building. Include one missing-information case, one duplicate, one malicious instruction and one failed external service. State where customer information lives and who may access it. Keep the UI small: a list of pending leads, detail view, evidence and approve or reject controls.

**Worked example**

A repair shop owner opens a pending lead, sees the extracted issue and the proposed reply, checks the source message, edits the draft and approves it.

**Try it**

Complete the project brief in projects/01-lead-management.md and decide what a 2-minute demo must show.

**Key point**

One coherent lead journey; explicit demo and acceptance criteria.
