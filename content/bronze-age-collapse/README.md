# Bronze Age Collapse

The Late Bronze Age system before, during and after collapse, studied through evidence, competing explanations and historical method.

Central question: was the collapse a single event, or a cascading failure of an interconnected system? Distinguishes primary evidence, consensus, likely and contested interpretation, and speculation.
